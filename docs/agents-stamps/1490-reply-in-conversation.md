#1490 on `feat/1490-reply-in-conversation` (2026-10-14, base `211e87807`, PR TBD): a message written in a channel or thread is answered there, not in a DM — `Gateway.Conversation {channelId, threadId?, replyToMessageId?}` rides on `MessageTarget`, the gateway actor target and `DeliveryTarget` (`packages/protocol/src/gateway/{schema,message}.ts`); the channels router records the ingress conversation (`{channelId, threadId?, messageId, dm}` from the ingress facts) on the prompt's external origin (`router/message-execution.ts`), `messaging/authorize.ts` carries a conversation onto the delivery target only under a `surface` pin (unpinned = `target_stale`), and the delivery route takes it as a fourth argument (`message-ports.ts`, `provider/contract.ts`). Slack posts into `conversation.channelId` threaded under `threadId ?? replyToMessageId` instead of `conversations.open`; Telegram sends `reply_to_message_id` / `message_thread_id` (forum topic, `is_topic_message` + `message_thread_id` now parsed as `reply.threadId`); Discord/GitHub refuse a conversation hint with `DeliveryNotSent` (`support/deliver.ts refuseConversation`) rather than DMing anyway. A Slack mention at channel top level keys its ingress thread on its own `ts` so follow-ups under the answer land on the same session. The resident report (`apps/openomni/src/bundles/resident/index.ts`) maps a non-DM origin conversation to `{channelId, threadId?, replyToMessageId: messageId}` and passes it as `send_message` `to.contact.conversation` (schema field added in `bundles/messaging/send-message/index.ts`); a DM origin carries none, so DM behavior is unchanged.
